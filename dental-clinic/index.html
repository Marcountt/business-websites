How we work

Four things you can expect every visit

  • Time to talk

    Check-ups are booked for 45 minutes, so there's room for your questions.

  • Prices up front

    You get a written estimate before any treatment begins. No surprises at the desk.

  • Nervous? Say so

    Tell us when you book. We'll go slower, agree a stop signal and explain as we go.

  • Step-free access

    Ground-floor surgery, wide doors, and a quiet room if you need a moment.

First visit

What happens at your first appointment

  1. 1Before you arrive

    A short health form

    We email a two-minute form about medications and past treatment, so we don't spend your appointment on paperwork.

  2. 2About 10 minutes

    A conversation first

    Your dentist asks what's bothering you, if anything, and what you'd like from the visit.

  3. 3About 30 minutes

    Examination and X-rays if needed

    A full look at teeth and gums. You can see everything on the screen beside the chair.

  4. 4Before you leave

    A written plan with prices

    If anything needs doing, you get options and a written estimate to take home and think over.
